    The road to the Leonida platinum — five stages

    Built from the official trophy descriptions and the published roadmaps: finish the story first (there is no mission replay, so keep a manual save at the start of every chapter), sweep the strands and the 100% checklist next, then collections and the money-and-feats cleanup.
